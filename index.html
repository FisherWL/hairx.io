02 — Privacy by design

The photo helps create the moment. It doesn’t become the product.

01

Consent comes first

Customers review a plain-language privacy prompt and actively consent before a preview is generated.

02

Photos are ephemeral

The customer photo is used for the current experience and is designed to be deleted when the session ends.

03

No customer CRM

A customer name is optional; contact details are not collected through the shared kiosk.

04

Useful context remains

Generated looks and styling attributes may be retained to support the consultation the customer chose to send.

About HairX

Building a thoughtful bridge between inspiration and expertise.

HairX LLC is developing HairX, a salon consultation platform for customers, stylists, and modern shop teams. The company was founded by Qiaochun Wei and is based in Canton, Ohio.
